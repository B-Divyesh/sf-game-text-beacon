Game Text Beacon bundles the local Tesseract OCR engine and English language data
inside desktop packages. Tesseract and its English trained data are licensed
under Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/blob/main/LICENSE

The build copies only the dynamic libraries required by the platform-provided
Tesseract distribution. Their notices remain available from that distribution.

Linux packages also bundle eSpeak NG and its voice data so an AppImage or a
package-manager installation can speak without a host speech command. eSpeak
NG is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
